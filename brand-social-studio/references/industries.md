# Adapting by industry

The method never changes: study the account → find its post types → gather true hooks → plan one reference post per new post → build with real assets and HTML-set text → check by eye → deliver the presenter.

What changes is what counts as a "real asset", the post types and where the hooks come from.

| Industry | Real assets to use | Post types to look for | News and upcoming hooks |
|---|---|---|---|
| **SaaS / app** | Product UI screenshots (clean demo data), short screen recordings, team photos, customer logos (with permission), the changelog | Feature launch, how-to carousel, before/after UI, customer story with a metric, milestone (users, uptime), webinar or event card, hiring, integration announcement | Release notes, new integrations, conference talks, review-site badges (G2, Capterra), funding, product hunt launches, compliance (SOC 2) |
| **Digital marketing agency** | Approved client work, dashboards with data blurred, team at work, workshops | Case-study carousel (problem → approach → result with numbers), tips and myth-vs-fact carousels, platform-update explainers, team spotlight, award posts | Meta and Google platform changes, industry reports, awards, new client launches (approved), events |
| **D2C / retail** | Packshots, lifestyle shoots, UGC (with permission), store photos | Drop or launch, offer, how-to-use, UGC repost, bundle, festive greeting, restock | Sales calendar, new collections, collabs, store openings, festivals |
| **B2B / industrial / logistics** | Plant and site photos, drone footage, people at work (with permission), certificates | Milestone, partner welcome, certification, leadership or speaker card, press clipping, explainer carousel | Trade shows, policy and budget news, plant openings, MoUs, reports quoting the company |
| **Real estate** | Drone and site footage, approved renders, amenity photos, construction updates | Project and campaign carousels, construction update, sold-out or possession posts, festive greetings, events at the club or clubhouse | Launches and approvals (RERA), infrastructure news, festive offers, events |
| **Healthcare / clinics** | Facility photos, doctors (with consent), equipment | Awareness days, doctor speaks, service explainers, camp recaps | Health days, new departments, accreditations |
| **Education** | Campus, events, student achievements (with consent) | Admissions open, results, alumni stories, event recaps | Admission cycles, exams, rankings, fests |

**Watch-outs that apply to every industry:**
- Faces of real people only from the brand's own published material. Never put a person's photo next to someone else's name or quote.
- Regulated claims (health, finance, real-estate pricing) only from official sources, and carry the licence or registration numbers the brand itself shows (RERA, SEBI, etc.).
- Competitor and third-party names only when the brand itself publicly mentions them.
